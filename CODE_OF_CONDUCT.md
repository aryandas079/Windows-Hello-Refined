# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Give and receive actionable feedback in good faith.
- Focus on the issue, implementation, and user impact.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, hateful conduct, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and any retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not open a public issue containing private or sensitive details.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate to keep the project safe and constructive.
